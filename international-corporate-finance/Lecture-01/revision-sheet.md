## Investment Appraisal Methods — Quick Comparison

| Method | What It Does | Accounts for Time Value? | Decision Rule |
|--------|-------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| **Payback** | Time to recover investment | ✗ No | Shorter = better |
| **ARR** | Average profit % of investment | ✗ No | Higher = better |
| **NPV** | Today's value of future cash flows | ✓ Yes | NPV > 0 = invest |
| **IRR** | % return the project earns | ✓ Yes | IRR > WACC = invest |

> **Exam tip:** NPV is the theoretically superior method. If asked which is "best," say NPV — but explain why companies also use others (simplicity, communication to non-finance stakeholders).

## Cost of Capital Models — Quick Comparison

| Model | Factors Used | Complexity | Best Used When |
|-------|-------------|------------|----------------|
| **CAPM** | Risk-free rate + one market risk factor (Beta) | Medium | Straightforward equity valuation |
| **APM** | Risk-free rate + multiple macro risk factors | High | Complex international firms |
| **WACC** | Combines cost of equity (CAPM/APM) + cost of debt | Medium | Evaluating whole-company projects |

## Flash Card Questions

**Q: What does WACC stand for and what does it represent?**
A: Weighted Average Cost of Capital — the blended rate a company pays to fund itself (mix of debt + equity costs).

**Q: What is the CAPM formula?**
A: Expected Return = Risk-Free Rate + β × (Market Return − Risk-Free Rate)

**Q: What decision rule applies to NPV?**
A: If NPV > 0, accept the project. If NPV < 0, reject it.

**Q: What is the IRR decision rule?**
A: Accept if IRR > cost of capital (WACC). The higher the IRR above WACC, the better.

**Q: What are the three pillars of the Triple Bottom Line?**
A: People, Planet, Profit.

**Q: What is IFRS S2?**
A: The climate-specific international sustainability reporting standard — requires companies to disclose climate risks, emissions, and transition plans.

**Q: What are the two assessment components and their weightings?**
A: Verbal report (20%) and Written report (80%).

**Q: What does Beta (β) measure in CAPM?**
A: A stock's sensitivity to market movements. β > 1 means more volatile than the market; β < 1 means less volatile.

**Q: What is the main weakness of the Payback method?**
A: It ignores the time value of money and ignores cash flows after the payback period.

A: SDG 7, SDG 11, SDG 12, SDG 13.
