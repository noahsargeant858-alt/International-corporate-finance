This module puts you in the shoes of a financial analyst working on a **real-time case study based on an oil company**. ## 2. The Five Learning Outcomes

These are the five things you must be able to demonstrate by the end of the module. Everything — lectures, seminars, assessments — maps back to these.

### LO1 — Business Risk Identification & Strategy
> Identify business problems/risks and recommend strategies to reduce or eliminate them.

In plain English: look at a company (your oil company case study), spot what could go wrong financially and operationally, and propose solutions. Think currency risk, oil price volatility, political risk in foreign markets.

### LO2 — Sources of Finance & Cost of Capital
> Identify appropriate long-term and short-term sources of finance and calculate cost of capital using **WACC**, **CAPM** & **APM**.

Three frameworks you must master:
- **WACC** (Weighted Average Cost of Capital) — what it costs a company to fund itself using a blend of debt and equity
- **CAPM** (Capital Asset Pricing Model) — calculates required return on equity based on market risk
- **APM** (Arbitrage Pricing Model) — a more complex alternative to CAPM using multiple risk factors

### LO3 — Cash Flow Forecasting & Financial Modelling
> Forecast future cashflows and conduct "What-If Analysis" using Excel financial models.

You will build Excel models. This is hands-on. What-If Analysis means changing assumptions (e.g., "what if the oil price drops 20%?") and seeing how it affects the numbers.

### LO4 — International Capital Investment Decisions
> Analyse investment decisions using **Payback**, **ARR**, **NPV** & **IRR**.

Four investment appraisal techniques for evaluating whether a major international investment (CAPEX) is worth making:
- **Payback** — how long until you get your money back
- **ARR** (Accounting Rate of Return) — profit as a percentage of investment
- **NPV** (Net Present Value) — today's value of future cash flows minus the cost
- **IRR** (Internal Rate of Return) — the % return the project generates

### LO5 — Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A)
> Evaluate possible mergers and acquisitions, highlighting the risks involved.

Why companies merge, how to value a target company, and what can go wrong (spoiler: most M&As destroy value).

## 4. The Case Study Context

The case study centres on an **oil company** navigating:
- **Sustainable energy transition** — pressure to shift to renewable/clean energy mixes
- **Sustainability reporting** — IFRS S1 & S2 standards (new international sustainability disclosure requirements)
- **Elkington's Triple Bottom Line** — balancing People, Planet, and Profit (not just shareholder returns)
- **Climate action vs energy provision** — the tension between cutting emissions and still supplying energy

This links to four UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):

| SDG | Relevance |
|-----|-----------|
| SDG 7 — Affordable & Clean Energy | Core case study theme |
| SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities | Sustainable finance angle |
| SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption | Triple Bottom Line |
| SDG 13 — Climate Action | Oil company vs environment |

## 6. AI Usage Policy (TIER 2)

This module operates under **TIER 2** AI guidelines. The framework is:

| Pillar | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Process** | You must declare how you used AI and which tools |
| **People** | Your critical thinking and decisions must be clearly your own — AI can't think for you |
| **Product** | The final work must be genuinely yours; AI-assisted but not AI-generated |

**What you CAN use AI for:** research support, taking notes, brainstorming, mind mapping, summarising, presentations, information search, complex searches

**What you CANNOT use AI for:** writing sections of your report, data analysis, problem-solving steps, systematic literature reviews, data visualisation
