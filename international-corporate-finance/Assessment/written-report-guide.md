# Written Report — Guide & Structure
**Submission: 1pm Monday 14 December 2026 | 3,000 words ±10% | 80%**

---

## What Dr Osiolo Actually Wants (From the Brief)

> "I should need to read EVERY WORD"

She does NOT want:
- Definitions of NPV, IRR, WACC etc (she knows what they are)
- Explanations of how you did calculations ("I calculated NPV by discounting cashflows at the WACC...")
- Waffle intros ("In order to decide which option to recommend, I will...")
- Theory padding

She DOES want:
- **Clear recommendations** (state what you recommend, then justify it)
- **Evidence-based justification** (numbers, real-time data, credible sources)
- **Full evaluation of BOTH options** (the Board can't decide unless both are analysed)

---

## Suggested Structure

### Executive Summary (1–2 pages, NOT included in word count)
Write this **last**. Summarise:
- What you were asked to evaluate
- Your recommendation (Option 1 or 2)
- How you recommend it be financed
- Top 3 reasons for your recommendation
- Top 3 risks

This is what the Board reads first. If it's unclear, you've failed before page 1.

---

### Section 1: Strategic Context (brief — ~200 words)
Not a definition section. One short section connecting UK Oil's strategic position to the decision:
- Current capital structure and what that means for financing new investment
- Oil sector conditions (oil price, UK North Sea outlook, energy transition pressure)
- Why this investment decision matters now

> Link to real-time oil prices and Bank of England rate data here.

### Section 2: Option 1 — North Sea Reservoir (~900 words)

**2.1 Recommendation** ← State it first
"Recommend proceeding with Option 1, financed by [method], using [supplier] for the platform and [supplier] for materials."

**2.2 Cash Flow Forecast**
- Include as an Excel appendix; summarise key assumptions in the text
- State your oil price assumption and source
- State your exchange rates (for EUR/GBP platform, USD/GBP materials)
- Apply your regression-derived production cost for 7M barrels
- Apply UK inflation to other costs
- Apply relevant tax allowances to CAPEX

**2.3 Investment Appraisal**
- Payback, ARR, NPV, IRR — present results in a table
- Compare against 15% hurdle rate
- State clearly: does the project add value?

**2.4 Supplier Recommendations**
- Platform: British Oil Machinery (£300M) vs Munchen (€355M)
  - Convert Munchen to £ using current EUR/GBP rate
  - Factor in FX risk and advance payment implications
- Materials: Russia vs USA vs Netherlands
  - Convert all to £/ton using current rates
  - Add documentary charges (0.25% vs 0.75%)
  - Factor in payment security and FX/geopolitical risk

**2.5 Finance Recommendation**
- Calculate WACC under each scenario (100% equity, 100% debt, combo)
- Present recommended financing mix with justification
- Consider: SPE structure? Rights issue pricing? Bond vs bank loan?
- Address tax shield on debt vs financial distress risk

**2.6 Risk Analysis**
- Critical path duration (PERT calculations for key activities)
- Flag Activity F (safety engineer shortage) — critical path risk
- Oil price sensitivity (what if price drops 15%/25%?)
- FX sensitivity on platform and materials

### Section 3: Option 2 — Arabic Oil Supplies M&A (~900 words)

**3.1 Recommendation** ← State it first
"Recommend [merger/acquisition] of Arabic Oil Supplies at [price], financed by [method]."

**3.2 Business Valuation**
Present multiple valuation methods in a table:
- **Book value:** Net assets = $273M (before premium)
- **Market value:** 250M shares × $1.20 = $300M
- **Earnings-based:** P/E multiple × net profit
- **Asset-based:** NCA $323M + NCA $100M - liabilities $150M
- Convert all to £ at current USD/GBP rate

**3.3 Merger vs Acquisition Analysis**
| | Merger (1:1 share exchange) | Acquisition (at £1.30/share) |
|-|-------|------|
| Cost to UK Oil | Dilution of existing shares | Cash/new shares at £1.30 |
| Goodwill | Calculated on agreed values | Market premium over book |
| Control | Shared (merged entity) | Full control (80-100%) |
| Finance needed | None (share swap) | Rights Issue or Debt |

**3.4 Financial Opportunities**
- Vertical integration savings (no longer buying materials from Russia/USA/Netherlands)
- Zero Abu Dhabi tax rate — quantify the annual tax saving
- Group Treasury management fees (£1.5M/year — cost or profit centre?)
- Transfer pricing opportunities between UK and Abu Dhabi entities

**3.5 Financial Problems**
- **Remittance restriction (30%)** — quantify how much profit is locked in Abu Dhabi annually
- FX risk on USD-denominated earnings
- Goodwill impairment risk if integration underperforms

**3.6 Finance Recommendation**
- Rights Issue: calculate value of rights (aim for > £1.00 per right)
- Or Debt: calculate WACC impact
- Consider: does acquisition change UK Oil's overall gearing to unacceptable levels?

## Word Count — What Doesn't Count
Per the brief, these are excluded from the 3,000 words:
- Table of contents
- Executive summary
- References
- Charts, tables, and images

> **Practical implication:** Put your cash flow model, PERT chart, and appraisal results in tables/appendices. Discuss the findings in prose. You get all the analysis without burning word count on numbers.

## The Marker's Test

If no → delete it.
If yes → keep it, make sure there's a number behind it.
